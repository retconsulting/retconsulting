Turn the dials.

Dials is a manual camera for iPhone. Set shutter speed and ISO by hand, choose a film, and keep your photos on your device.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Exposure, by hand.

Shutter speed and ISO each have their own dial, with haptic clicks. Turn back to the red A and the camera takes over.

See the film before the shot.

Ten film looks run live in the viewfinder, so you judge the picture as you frame it. On supported hardware, the RAW file stays free of the film.

Read the light.

Histogram, zebras and focus peaking show what the sensor receives. Turn on what the picture needs and leave the rest off.
