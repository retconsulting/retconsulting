Availability

Dials is not on sale yet. It will be a paid app, distributed through the App Store. The price, the date and the countries will appear on its App Store page.

Licence

Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement will govern the use of Dials downloaded from the App Store.

What the app does

Available formats, resolutions, lenses, flash and depth depend on the iPhone. Each camera module has a fixed physical aperture. Portrait simulation is a software effect that uses depth data. Film looks are processing choices and do not reproduce a specific film stock.
