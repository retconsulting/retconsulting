How do I enable the camera?

In iPhone Settings, open Apps, then Dials and enable camera access. If Dials displays “Open Settings”, tap it to open the app’s settings.

Where can I find my photo?

Open the Dials gallery and inspect the capture details. “Saved to Photos” means that Dials saved a copy to Apple Photos. If saving failed, check the permission to add photos and the available storage before retrying. Manage copies in Dials and in Photos separately.

How do I keep my originals?

Before deleting Dials or changing iPhones, check the images in Photos or export the files from the gallery to a destination you keep. Dials asks iOS to exclude its gallery folder from device backups.

Why is a format unavailable?

Available formats depend on the iPhone, lens and camera mode. Standard Bayer raw capture uses the camera’s standard resolution, typically 12 megapixels. Apple ProRAW requires compatible hardware.

What should I include in a support request?

Include your iPhone model, iOS and Dials versions, the steps you took and the message shown. A description usually suffices. If an image helps, use a sample or hide private details.
