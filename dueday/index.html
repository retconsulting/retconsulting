What does dueday do?

dueday gathers your recurring costs and shows what is left to pay this month, each with its own countdown.

What about my data?

It stays on your device. dueday works offline, with no account and no bank connection.

  • 100% offlineNothing leaves your device.
  • No accountNo signup, no email.
  • No data collectedNo ads, no trackers.
  • No bankNo bank connection.
Read the privacy policy

What about shared costs?

Solo, Duo and Pro keep your personal, shared and business costs apart. Each space has its own costs, budget, currency and colour.

Can I see everything at a glance?

Yes. Each payment sits on its day in the calendar, and Insights compares the month’s total with your budget and income.

Does it cost anything?

No. Every core feature is free.

On all my devices?

Yes. dueday runs on iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad and Mac with Apple silicon.
