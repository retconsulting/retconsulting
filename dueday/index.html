Promises

What dueday does for you.

Complete privacy

100% offline: nothing leaves your device. No data collected, ever. Not a single byte goes through a server.

No account

No signup, no email. Open the app and start.

Free, no limits

Every core feature, free and with no time limit. Only optional, purely cosmetic extras could ever become paid.

The screens

Three screens, everything's there.

Home

The essentials at a glance

Your next payments, the monthly total and all your cost cards, sortable by due date, amount, name or category.

Calendar

Every payment on its day

Each due date pinned to its date, the detail one tap away, and two quiet reminders: the day before and on the day.

Analytics

Your spending in perspective

Monthly total, breakdown by category, your top 5 costs, measured against your budget and your income.

Behind the product

A product built end to end.

The need
Knowing what you pay each month, with no spreadsheet to maintain and no account to create. The brief fit in one sentence: see everything, be warned in time.
The choices
100% offline rather than a server: simpler, more reliable, and privacy is settled by design. No account, no ads, free.
The delivery
Scoping, design, development and App Store prep handled in-house by RET Consulting, from first sketch to final build.
