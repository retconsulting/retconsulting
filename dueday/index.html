    Questions

    Is dueday free?

    Yes. Every core feature is free. Only optional, cosmetic extras could ever become paid.

    Does it connect to my bank?

    No. You enter your costs yourself. dueday does not pay anything and does not verify that a payment was made.

    Which devices does it need?

    iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later, or a Mac with Apple silicon, which runs the iPad app.

    In which languages?

    English and French.

    How do I keep my data?

    Export a backup file from Settings and keep it where you choose. More in support.

    Is what I see here the current version?

    This page shows the design of dueday 2.0. The App Store version can lag behind while an update is on its way.
