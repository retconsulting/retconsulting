The document, as a book

Book mode turns a Markdown file into a paginated book, full screen. Scroll to turn the page.

  • A cover, a table of contents and chapter openings.
  • Running headers, page numbers and an end page.
  • Short tables stay whole on a page; long ones repeat their header.
  • Turn by finger, trackpad, keys or a corner, with a real page curl.
  • On Mac, export exactly these pages as a PDF book.

The source or the page, your choice

Press Modifier, or Command-E, and two panes open: the Markdown on the left, the rendered page on the right. Type in either. Each updates the other.

Slash menu

Type / on either side to insert common blocks.

Saved as you go

Changes save automatically, in plain .md files.

Complex blocks

Mathematics, diagrams and callouts are edited in the source.

Ink or paper

Plain files, on all your devices

Your documents stay ordinary .md files that you can open anywhere.

iCloud Drive

Keep files in iCloud Drive, with synced recent documents and reading settings when iCloud is available.

Quick Look

Preview .md files in Finder and Files with the same rendering engine.

Export

On Mac: PDF, HTML, PNG, EPUB or print. On iPhone and iPad: the system share sheet.

Your documents stay yours

Reading or editing a document does not send it to RET Consulting. Emdee has no account, no advertising and no third-party analytics. iCloud features use Apple’s services, and a document with remote images contacts those image servers. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

When does Emdee come out?

Soon, on the App Store, for Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Is it free?

Yes. Emdee is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Which systems does it need?

macOS 13 or later on Mac, iOS 26 or later on iPhone and iPad.

Is the interface in English?

Not yet. The interface is in French. Your documents can be in any language.

Does it change my files?

It opens your .md files and saves them as Markdown. Keep a separate copy before testing edits or synchronisation.

Can it edit everything on the page?

Paragraphs, headings, lists, tasks, quotes, code and table cells, yes. Mathematics and diagrams are edited in the source pane.
