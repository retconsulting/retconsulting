The same .md file, on Mac, iPad and iPhone.

##Read it as a page.

Open any .md file: real typography, a table of contents, search, footnotes and sidenotes. Tables, tasks, mathematics and diagrams come out clean.

##Edit it directly.

Tap Modifier, or press Command-E on Mac: the source on one side, the page on the other. Type in either, and each updates the other. Your files stay plain .md.

##Make it a book.

Book mode sets your document as pages, full screen: a cover, a table of contents, chapter openings, running headers and page numbers. On Mac, export these pages as a PDF book.

##Follow your projects.

Dates, @mentions, #tags, statuses and priorities become chips you read at a glance. Each date tells you how many days are left.
