Emdee privacy policy

Updated · 7 November 2026

Emdee opens the documents you choose. Reading or editing a document does not send it to RET Consulting. The app requires no Emdee account and contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

Documents and settings

Emdee stores its cache, file-access permissions and reading settings on your device. You choose where to save your documents.

When iCloud is enabled, Emdee uses Apple's iCloud Drive for documents and Apple's key-value storage for recent-document information and reading settings.

Recent-document records include the document title (the first level-one heading, or the file name), file name, folder/location, local path, iCloud-relative path when applicable, a short plain-text excerpt of the first paragraph (up to 160 characters), opening date, reading position, device name and an app-generated device identifier. When iCloud synchronization is available, these records can be synchronized even for a document stored only on one device. The file itself is not uploaded by this recent-document synchronization; another device displays an unavailable local file as such. File-access bookmarks remain on the original device. Apple processes this information under your Apple Account and its applicable terms. Local-only builds without iCloud entitlements do not provide this synchronization. Manage iCloud through Apple's settings. Removing the app does not necessarily delete documents kept in Files, iCloud Drive or other folders.

Network content and other applications

Documents can contain remote images. Rendering such images can contact their servers and expose ordinary network information, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, to those servers. External links open through the system. Their destinations apply their own privacy rules. Local rendering of Markdown does not require a developer server.

On Mac, the explicit Open in Claude or ChatGPT command passes the file to an installed application. If it is not installed, Emdee copies the document text to the clipboard and opens the service's website; you choose whether to paste or send it. Sharing and exporting can give other applications or people access to your content. These are user-directed actions, not background uploads by Emdee.

Diagnostics

Emdee keeps up to 30 crash and performance reports on your device. It does not automatically send these reports to RET Consulting. If you join a TestFlight beta, Apple separately shares usage and crash data with RET Consulting to help diagnose faults. RET Consulting uses these reports in its legitimate interest in fixing beta issues and keeps individual copies only as long as needed to investigate them. You can also choose to send feedback. Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice.

Contact and support

RET Consulting SRL publishes Emdee. It cannot retrieve your documents from your device or your iCloud account. If you email us, we receive the message and any files you attach. A description or a fictional sample usually suffices.
