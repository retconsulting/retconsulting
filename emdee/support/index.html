Emdee support

Emdee is a free Markdown reader and editor for macOS 13 or later and iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later. The interface is French.

Contact

Email retbaz@retconsulting.be. Include your device, operating system version, Emdee version and build, and the steps that caused the issue. Send a non-sensitive example rather than a confidential document.

Start reading and editing

Choose Ouvrir to open a Markdown file. Choose Modifier to edit the source or the rendered page. Use Mode livre for a read-only paginated view. Close book mode with the × control. Complex blocks, such as diagrams and mathematics, are edited in the source pane.

Files and iCloud

Keep a separate copy before testing edits or synchronization. iCloud features need an Apple Account, iCloud Drive and an iCloud-enabled app build. On Mac, grant the selected folder when related local images or linked documents need access.

Export and share

On Mac, Fichier > Exporter offers document export formats. On iPhone and iPad, Partager shares the Markdown file through the system share sheet. Quick Look does not run Mermaid diagrams; open them in Emdee.
