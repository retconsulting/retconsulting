Engagement

Method

From the first scoping workshop to the final review, each step produces a deliverable your team owns.

  1. Discover 01

    Co-creation workshop

    Mapping the needs, processes and constraints specific to your team, to turn scattered requests into work streams ranked by impact and effort. Sponsor and key people around the table.

    Deliverable A prioritised opportunity map, produced in session.
  2. Frame 02

    Agenda and mandate

    Turning the workshop into one short executable plan: measurable objectives, a precise scope, milestones, roles and responsibilities on your side, success criteria. A clean exit point: the mandate stays executable without me.

    Deliverable An executable mandate that remains yours, even if the engagement stops there.
  3. Deliver 03

    Delivery

    Running the chosen RET Learning format on your own tools and approved data, to turn practical exercises and feedback into a final demonstration or review.

    Deliverable A final review against the success criteria, and a documented handover.
