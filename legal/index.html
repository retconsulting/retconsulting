Privacy

This website sets no cookies and uses no audience measurement or advertising trackers. GitHub Pages hosts the pages. When you visit, GitHub records your Internet Protocol (IP) address for security. GitHub Pages hosting documentation.

The contact form opens a draft in your email app. You choose whether to send it.

If you send an email, RET Consulting SRL receives your address, your message and any attachments. Its email provider delivers and stores the correspondence. RET Consulting uses it to answer you and to resolve the issue you report. RET Consulting SRL is the controller for these exchanges. Its legal basis is its legitimate interest in handling enquiries. It retains the correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose.

Your rights when RET Consulting receives personal data

For example, if you emailed support, you can ask RET Consulting SRL for a copy of your message or ask it to correct or erase your personal data. Write to retbaz@retconsulting.be.

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) also lets you request restricted use of your data in certain cases. You may object, for reasons relating to your situation, when RET Consulting relies on its legitimate interests. Portability applies to data you provided when automated processing is based on consent or a contract. You can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including the Belgian Data Protection Authority.
